The problem

Repair & maintenance in Bangladesh runs on blind trust

Millions of vehicles and home appliances are serviced every day by informal workshops. The owner usually has no idea what was done, what it should cost, or whether the parts were genuine.

Unfair & random pricing

Mechanics often charge different prices to different customers for the same work.

Fake or unchanged parts

Owners cannot verify whether a part was actually replaced or if it was genuine.

No service history

Paper receipts get lost. When selling a car or bike, there is no proof of maintenance.

Hard to find trusted help

No easy way to find a verified, well-rated provider near your home.
