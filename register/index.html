Step 1 · Registration

Create your Trust Pass account

Choose whether you need a service (receiver) or you offer a service (provider).

What happens here?
  • Two types of users can register: receivers and providers.
  • A provider's profile (name, services, location, visit charge) becomes visible on the Find Providers page.
  • The receiver's area is used to calculate distance and sort providers near → far.

Provider verification process

  1. NID & trade licence check
  2. Phone OTP verification
  3. Skill test / workshop visit
  4. Verified ✔ badge on profile
