About Trust Pass Ltd.

Know your asset, trust your service.

Trust Pass: A Portable Digital Service Passport for Transparent Repair and Maintenance in Bangladesh.

Mission

Bring transparency, fair pricing and accountability to the repair and maintenance sector of Bangladesh.

Vision

Every vehicle and appliance in Bangladesh has a trusted digital service history that travels with it.

Target users

Car & bike owners, households, small offices — and the thousands of independent mechanics and technicians.

Technology used

  • Next.js 15 (App Router) — React framework for pages and routing
  • TypeScript — type-safe code
  • Tailwind CSS — responsive, modern styling
  • React Context — application state management
  • Lucide icons — icon set

Future scope

  • Cloud infrastructure — Scalable servers to support users across all of Bangladesh.
  • Payment gateway integration — bKash, Nagad and SSLCommerz for real payments and automatic payouts.
  • Live GPS tracking — Track the provider on a map as they travel to you.
  • Mobile app & Bangla language — Android / iOS apps with full Bangla support.
  • Tamper-proof records — Digitally signed passport entries that cannot be edited.
  • Partnerships — BRTA, insurance companies and used-car marketplaces.
